# Clause architecture

## Definition

The working clause representation distinguishes Subject from Predicate and then analyzes the predicate's head, complements and modifiers. Complete constituent spans are needed before functions are assigned. A main clause can contain other clauses rather than excluding them from its own extent.

## Worked example

In "The careful researcher placed the samples on the table yesterday", the Subject is the complete NP "the careful researcher". The Predicate includes placed and all three following dependents. The samples is Object; on the table is a selected locative complement; yesterday is a temporal modifier.

## Distinctions

Main-clause core is not the same as the full main clause including subordinate material. A flattened list of words loses the nesting of the table inside the PP.

## Scope and limits

This is a descriptive constituency model. TP/CP structures, dependency grammars and analyses of adjunct attachment may represent the same data differently. Word order is evidence, not the entire syntax.
