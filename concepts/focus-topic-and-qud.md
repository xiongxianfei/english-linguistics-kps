## Definition

Topic concerns what a discourse stretch is about; a question under discussion is the issue being addressed; focus relates an expression or domain to relevant alternatives. Givenness concerns accessibility. These are interacting dimensions rather than synonyms.

## Worked example

For "Who repaired the server?", "ANNA repaired the server" has narrow Subject focus Anna and background "__ repaired the server". For "What did Anna repair?", the focused NP is the server. For "What did Anna do?", a broader VP focus can share strongest accent on server.

## Distinctions

Focus is not always new; a previously mentioned alternative can be contrastively focused. Subject need not be Topic, and the accented word need not exhaust the focus domain.

## Scope and limits
