# Discourse referent

## Key takeaway
A discourse referent is a representation used to track an individual, group, event or other object through discourse.

## Summary

## Definition

A discourse referent is a representation used to track an individual, group, event or other object through discourse. It is not the word introducing it and does not by itself establish a corresponding actual-world entity.

## Worked example

"Maya bought a laptop. It was expensive" introduces a laptop referent l and adds properties to l. "The machine" may later retrieve l. In "Maya imagined a dragon", a discourse representation can support discussion of a dragon without asserting that a real dragon exists.

## Distinctions

The same word may introduce different referents, and different expressions may track one referent. Repeating an indefinite a dog does not force identity with a previously mentioned dog.

## Scope and limits

Accessibility depends on structure: indefinites under negation or quantification do not always become freely available to following pronouns. This introductory record is not a complete Discourse Representation Theory implementation.
