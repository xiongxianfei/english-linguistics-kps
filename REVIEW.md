# Review policy

## Key takeaway
Editorial review, source inspection and independent scientific review are different records.

## Summary
Every linguistic knowledge entity and extracted summary claim remains provisional. The review queue exposes body claims not yet extracted and the distinction between context-only source links and claim-level support.

## Promotion
A version-specific review must name an actual reviewer, inspected claims, passages, limitations and findings. No automated tool establishes that a review occurred. See [AUTHORING](AUTHORING.md), [CHECKS](CHECKS.md) and [the review queue](REVIEW-QUEUE.md).
