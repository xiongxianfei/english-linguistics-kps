# Foundation coverage

## Key takeaway
Ten subsystem Models explain internal structure and major interfaces without claiming exhaustive coverage.

## Summary
This is an optional catalog of canonical Markdown, not another knowledge store. Each item has a local summary here; its full explanation, examples and limits remain in its owning file. All linguistic content is provisional.

## Domain entry points

| Domain | Local summary | Model |
|---|---|---|
| discourse | Discourse analysis connects utterances through referents, coherence relations and interaction while distinguishing text evidence from inferred interpretation. | [English discourse system](models/english-discourse-system.md) |
| information-structure | Information structure distinguishes topic, focus, givenness and the question under discussion rather than equating them with grammatical positions or accented words. | [English information structure system](models/english-information-structure-system.md) |
| lexicon | The lexicon connects lexical identity, form, senses and combinatorial possibilities; it is not merely a list of spellings and translations. | [English lexicon system](models/english-lexicon-system.md) |
| morphology | Morphology relates word-internal structure, grammatical features and lexical identity without equating morphemes with letters or syllables. | [English morphology system](models/english-morphology-system.md) |
| phonetics | Phonetics describes speech production, the acoustic signal and perception; a written transcription is an analysis, not a recording. | [English phonetics system](models/english-phonetics-system.md) |
| phonology | Phonology models contrast, distribution and the organization of sound patterns within a specified language variety. | [English phonology system](models/english-phonology-system.md) |
| pragmatics | Pragmatic analysis separates contextual resolution, discourse background and defeasible reasoning about what a speaker is doing. | [English pragmatics system](models/english-pragmatics-system.md) |
| prosody | Prosody organizes prominence, phrasing, timing and intonation across segments; no single pitch movement uniquely determines meaning. | [English prosody system](models/english-prosody-system.md) |
| semantics | Semantic analysis distinguishes conventional meaning, compositional structure, reference and inference under an explicitly fixed interpretation. | [English semantics system](models/english-semantics-system.md) |
| syntax | Syntax organizes categories, constituent structure, dependencies and grammatical functions; word order alone does not specify the whole analysis. | [English syntax system](models/english-syntax-system.md) |

## Interfaces

An interface Model specifies two analytical representations, a scoped relationship, a worked contrast and limits. Fourteen Models cover selected links among the ten domains; this does not imply that every pair is connected or that the arrows are causal processing stages. The subsystem Models explain these connections inline.

## Deliberate limits

The foundation does not exhaust accent inventories, acoustic measurement, lexical exceptions, formal derivations, corpus frequencies, discourse frameworks, variation, acquisition or historical change. A topic being named or linked does not establish scientific adequacy. Claim-specific external support and independent review remain incomplete.

## One complete case

[One passage across ten domains](models/one-passage-ten-domains.md) separates the word forms, clause functions, participant roles, reference choices, focus alternatives and predicted sound patterns of one constructed passage. No audio means no measured phonetic result.
