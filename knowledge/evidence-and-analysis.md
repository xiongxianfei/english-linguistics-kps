## Definition

An attested token, a constructed example, an elicited judgment, a source statement and an analyst's model are different evidence objects. Scientific documentation records what supports a claim, what is inferred, and what remains untested.

## Worked example

An invented example can demonstrate the internal consequence of a stated model. It cannot establish population frequency. A passed unit test can show that a plural-selection function matches its declared cases, not that the function explains every English plural.
