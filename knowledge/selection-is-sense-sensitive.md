## Statement

Knowing a word's lexical category alone does not determine all of its allowable complement patterns.

## Explanation

Verbs share distributional properties while differing in sense-specific argument relations. These differences constrain allowable syntactic realizations. The number of semantic participants alone also underdetermines whether a role is realized as an NP, PP or clause.

## Worked contrast

RUN in "Maya ran quickly" describes locomotion; RUN in "Maya ran the laboratory" describes management. DEPEND licenses an on-PP in the relevant sense. DISCUSS licenses an NP Object in the ordinary transitive construction used here.

## Scope and counterevidence
