## Representation

"Although the roads were icy, Anna sent the reports that Maya had prepared to Leo. He thanked her because he needed them."

| Level | Analysis |

| Syntax | Five finite clauses; relative clause is that Maya had prepared __ |
| Semantics | Anna: Agent; reports: Theme; Leo: Recipient; preparation earlier |
| Pragmatics | He favors Leo, her favors Anna, them favors reports |
| Discourse | Concession, prior preparation, reason for thanks |
| Information structure | With who-sent QUD, ANNA is focus; Topic needs wider context |

## Worked interpretation

The Direct Object is the complete NP the reports that Maya had prepared. To Leo attaches to sent in the intended analysis. Leo is NP complement of TO, not a Direct Object of SEND. The clause "because he needed them" explains the thanks; a further claim that Anna acted altruistically is only a plausible inference.
