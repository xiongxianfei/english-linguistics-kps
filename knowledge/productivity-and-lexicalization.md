## Definition

Productivity concerns the extendability of a pattern to suitable new bases. Lexicalization concerns the conventional status or specialized properties of a particular expression. They are not opposite ends of one scale.

## Worked example

The -ness pattern permits many adjective-to-noun formations. HAPPINESS is nevertheless an established lexeme. GREENHOUSE is structurally analyzable but has a conventional meaning not predicted by simply intersecting GREEN and HOUSE.

## Distinctions

Lexicalization does not mean that only some derivations are legal. A novel formation can be well formed but not established; an established form can have idiosyncratic properties.

## Scope and limits

Productivity is graded and sensitive to category, phonology, meaning and usage. No corpus productivity measure was calculated for this draft. Familiarity judgments alone should not be reported as a measured productivity score.
