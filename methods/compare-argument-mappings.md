# Compare argument mappings

## Key takeaway
Separate participant roles from grammatical functions when comparing constructions.

## Summary
Separate participant roles from grammatical functions when comparing constructions. The definitions and worked cases below state the intended analysis and its limits. This initial object is provisional; links deepen the explanation but do not replace it.

## Objective

Separate participant roles from grammatical functions when comparing constructions.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Two related sentences in a specified lexical sense and variety. Identify whether the comparison concerns passive, dative or causative alternation rather than assuming equivalence.

## Rationale

A syntactic Subject is not permanently an Agent. Mapping roles and functions in separate columns makes it possible to tell role preservation from the addition of a causal participant.

## Procedure

1. Identify each lexical predicate and its intended sense.
2. List expressed and plausibly implicit participants without inventing referents.
3. Assign semantic roles with definitions and acknowledged alternatives.
4. Analyze grammatical functions and local PP structure independently.
5. Compare the two mappings.
6. State which content remains stable and which changes, including possible construction-specific meaning differences.

## Worked example

"Leo gave Maya a letter" assigns Maya Recipient and Indirect Object. "Maya was given a letter by Leo" assigns Maya Recipient and Subject. In "A letter was given to Maya", Maya is NP complement of TO. None of these positions makes Maya Patient.

## Expected effect

The output is an explicit, revisable analysis with its assumptions and uncertainties exposed. This is a design expectation, not a measured learning outcome.

## Validation and counterevidence

The role inventory and participant identities must stay explicit. Test whether a causative comparison adds an Agent/Causer; do not classify it as mere movement of the same arguments. Counterexamples to a proposed generalization should remain visible.

## Limits

Role inventories differ, and dative alternants are not unrestricted paraphrases. A narrow worked contrast does not license all verbs in all alternations.
