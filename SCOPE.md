# Scope and theory policy

## Key takeaway
This release is a foundational, theory-aware map of English linguistic representations, not an exhaustive grammar or certified scientific reference.

## Summary
The package covers ten domains and major interfaces at an introductory analytical level. It preserves alternative analyses and explicit variety limits rather than presenting one framework as universal.

## Included

Foundational distinctions in Phonetics, Phonology, Morphology, Lexicon, Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics, Discourse, Information Structure and Prosody; reusable Principles and Models; analysis Methods; operational Practices; selected source records; and generated coverage/interface views.

## Variety policy

General American remains the primary reference variety for new pronunciation examples under [PRONUNCIATION](PRONUNCIATION.md). Other English varieties remain legitimate and must be labeled rather than silently normalized. No variety is treated as socially superior.

## Theory policy

NP/Subject/Predicate terminology and descriptive constituent/dependency analyses are working choices. Alternative DP/CP/TP, constructional, functional, constraint-based and other analyses may distribute explanatory work differently. Semantic role inventories, discourse frameworks, focus theories and phonological representations are likewise theory-sensitive.

## Exclusions and gaps

This foundation does not exhaust sociolinguistics, language acquisition, historical change, corpus methods, psycholinguistic processing, clinical linguistics, computational linguistics or detailed formal derivations. Newly added coverage-gap objects remain provisional introductions, not completed specialist treatments.

## Scientific boundary

Examples are constructed unless explicitly identified otherwise. Structural validation checks identities, links, metadata, views and declared mappings; it does not establish truth, prevalence, speaker behavior, pedagogical effectiveness or expert approval.
