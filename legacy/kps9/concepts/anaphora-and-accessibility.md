# Anaphora and accessibility

## Key takeaway
Anaphora links an expression's interpretation to discourse material or a structurally available antecedent.

## Summary
Anaphora links an expression's interpretation to discourse material or a structurally available antecedent. The definitions and worked cases below state the intended analysis and its limits. This initial object is provisional; links deepen the explanation but do not replace it.

## Definition

Anaphora links an expression's interpretation to discourse material or a structurally available antecedent. Accessibility involves recency, grammatical structure, topic, semantics and discourse organization. No single nearest-noun rule resolves every pronoun.

## Worked example

"A researcher entered. She sat down. The scientist opened a folder" can maintain one referent through several descriptions. "Maya placed the phone beside the notebook. Ravi charged it" usually favors the phone if notebook means a paper notebook; a laptop reading changes the evidence.

## Distinctions

An anaphoric chain is not a chain of identical meanings. A reference to the same microscope as the instrument is a coreferential redescription, whereas house to kitchen normally introduces a related but distinct referent through bridging.

## Scope and limits

Coreference, bound-variable interpretation and deixis are not identical. Gender and number cues interact with speaker usage and context; the draft does not implement a deterministic coreference resolver.
