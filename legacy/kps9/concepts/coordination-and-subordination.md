# Coordination and subordination

## Key takeaway
Coordination combines expressions of comparable syntactic status; subordination makes a clause dependent within a larger structure.

## Definition

Coordination combines expressions of comparable syntactic status; subordination makes a clause dependent within a larger structure. Neither label specifies how important the information is. Both mechanisms may appear together in one sentence.

## Worked example

"Maya read the report and wrote the summary" can coordinate VPs sharing a Subject. "Maya left because Ravi called" contains a reason subordinate clause. "The report that Maya wrote disappeared" embeds a relative clause within the Subject NP.

## Distinctions

And does not guarantee coordination of complete clauses; the exact conjunct spans matter. A dependent clause can carry the most important news, so structural dependence is not informational insignificance.

## Scope and limits

Coordination of unlike categories and shared material needs additional theory. Some grammars analyze subordinating words as prepositions taking clause complements; our adverbial-clause terminology is a declared pedagogical convention.
