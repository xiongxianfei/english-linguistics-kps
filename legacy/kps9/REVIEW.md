# Review policy

## Key takeaway
Evidence status is independent of publication status and automated validation.

## Summary
Evidence status is independent of publication status and automated validation. The initial broad release remains provisional; supporting links and checks do not constitute independent scientific review.

## Status vocabulary

All initial knowledge objects are draft and provisional. Source status separately records pending support or selected-source support. A merged PR can publish a provisional draft without converting it into reviewed science. Reviewed means a documented independent claim-level review, not a language model's self-evaluation.

## Review record

Record reviewer identity and actual role, date, object ID/version, claims inspected, full source identity, precise locators, access extent, scope, counterexamples, unresolved disagreements and decision. Do not fabricate these fields or copy the date of access as a publication date.

## Evidence granularity

A reference supports a particular statement, not every neighboring paragraph. Constructed examples illustrate a proposed analysis; tests protect their consistency but do not turn them into observations. Revisions should preserve the distinction between empirical findings, formal reasoning, definitional convention and architectural choice.

## Queue

The [review queue](REVIEW-QUEUE.md) lists all provisional objects and missing external support. Selected abstract inspection is insufficient for detailed formal claims. Independent reviewer availability is not assumed.
