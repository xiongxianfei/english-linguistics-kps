# Analyze complete utterance

## Key takeaway
Produce a multi-level analysis whose representations remain distinct and whose interfaces are explicit.

## Summary
Produce a multi-level analysis whose representations remain distinct and whose interfaces are explicit. The definitions and worked cases below state the intended analysis and its limits. This initial object is provisional; links deepen the explanation but do not replace it.

## Objective

Produce a multi-level analysis whose representations remain distinct and whose interfaces are explicit.

## Inputs and prerequisites

An utterance or passage, context and variety where available, and a record of what was actually observed. Use the seven passes as a review order, not a theory of brain processing.

## Rationale

An expression can simultaneously have morphological, syntactic, semantic, referential and prosodic properties. Independent annotations expose category errors and allow shared phenomena to be explained without collapsing the layers.

## Procedure

1. Record expression, word forms and inflectional features.
2. Identify lexical senses and selected complements.
3. Mark complete syntactic spans, local heads and functions.
4. Represent predicates, roles, scope and temporal interpretation.
5. Resolve context-sensitive values and label defeasible inferences.
6. Track discourse relations, QUD, givenness and focus.
7. Add accent-qualified phonological predictions or observed phonetic data.
8. Compare layers, document interface mappings and list unresolved alternatives.

## Worked example

In the integrated Anna passage, five finite clauses coexist with several auxiliary forms. The relative "that Maya had prepared __" is contained within the reports NP; "to Leo" is higher. Anna is sender, reports Theme, Leo Recipient. Under a who-sent QUD, ANNA is focus, not the interrogative word who.

## Expected effect

The output is an explicit, revisable analysis with its assumptions and uncertainties exposed. This is a design expectation, not a measured learning outcome.

## Validation and counterevidence

Check complete spans, one local head per stated relation, independent role/function fields, sound-conditioned suffixes and explicit/inferred distinction. The fixture tests protect this proposed analysis; they do not assess a learner or establish an exhaustive English grammar.

## Limits

Not every level is recoverable from writing alone. Leave prosody, speaker intent or reference unresolved when data are missing. Different theories may produce different compatible representations.
