# IPA diacritics and other symbols

## Key takeaway
Diacritics and tie bars add information that must not be lost when classifying a phonetic transcription.

## Summary
This record covers the [diacritics panel](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/IPAcharts/IPA_charts_E/images/IPA_Kiel_D_0600.png) and [other symbols panel](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/IPAcharts/IPA_charts_E/images/IPA_Kiel_OS_0600.png) of the official IPA chart. Their limited contribution is symbol interpretation, not empirical verification of American pronunciation or a learning routine.

## Publication identity
Publisher: International Phonetic Association. The [official chart overview](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/content/ipa-chart) links these panels. Accessed October 11, 2026. The displayed annual chart issue is not treated as proof of a particular webpage publication date.

## Inspection extent
The two PNG panels were visually inspected. The pulmonic-consonant and vowel panels were also consulted to distinguish how their paired-symbol conventions differ. No audio control, learner attempt or acoustic measurement was inspected.

## Diacritics
The diacritics panel explicitly identifies aspiration, voicelessness, syllabicity, nasalization and rhoticity as different modifications. A phonetic analysis should preserve the mark rather than treating every modified symbol as its unmodified base. Thus a devoicing mark and an aspiration mark do not encode the same property; syllabic marking does not reclassify a nasal consonant as a vowel.

## Other symbols
The other-symbol panel lists [w] as a voiced labial-velar approximant and notes that affricates and double articulations can use two symbols joined by a tie bar. Printed symbol count alone is therefore not a reliable count of independently articulated units.

## Scope and limits

This supplements the [existing official chart record](international-phonetic-association-undated-ipa-chart.md). These panels belong to the same IPA publication family, not independent corroborating studies.
Chart conventions define the intended symbol values. They do not establish which IPA symbol best fits an unknown recording. The Practices' selection, imitation and feedback sequence is original procedure design, not a finding reported by this chart.
