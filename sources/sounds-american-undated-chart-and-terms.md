# Sounds American chart and usage terms

## Key takeaway
Sounds American describes an instructional General American sound inventory and articulatory resources; it is not a verified transcript for every speaker or word.

## Summary
Source record for the [Sounds American website](https://soundsamerican.net/). The indexed American IPA chart lists representative vowel and consonant labels including /i/, /u/, /ə/, /ɚ/ and /ɝ/. The resource is used for *pedagogical articulation orientation*, not as evidence of token-specific realizations.

## Publication identity
[Website](https://soundsamerican.net/) and [terms of use](https://soundsamerican.net/article/terms_of_use). The website chart's publication date is undetermined; the terms page presents the date March 23, 2022.

## Inspection extent
On October 11, 2026, search-indexed chart text and the indexed terms-of-use document were consulted. The JavaScript-dependent lesson interface, individual sound videos, images and audio were not directly exercised. No claim is made that an original pronunciation performance was observed.

## Located contributions
The homepage describes its interactive chart as focusing on General American English and displays American-oriented IPA symbols. Its terms-of-use page describes general CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 conditions subject to specific exceptions.

## Limitations
Instructional IPA labels and demonstrations simplify accent diversity. Listed symbols are not sufficient evidence for precise English phoneme inventories, allophones, acoustic measurements, word-specific transcriptions or every American accent.
