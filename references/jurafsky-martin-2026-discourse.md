# Reference and conversation in Speech and Language Processing

## Key takeaway

The inspected passages supply introductory distinctions for tracking reference and organizing conversational responses; they do not provide a complete discourse theory or validate this package's examples.

## Summary

Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin's August 19, 2026 third-edition draft separates reference, discourse coherence and conversation into distinct chapters. Selected reference and conversation passages support the Discourse Model's treatment of mentions, referents, information status, adjacency and grounding. Conversation coverage is explicitly preliminary in the source. The Model's eight-area organization and connected examples are authored synthesis.

## Reference and information status

Selected extracted text on pages 1–6 distinguishes expressions that mention something from the discourse entities they evoke or access. It discusses coreference, reference to larger discourse contributions, and cases where an anaphoric expression does not simply designate one fixed earlier individual. The passages also distinguish prior mention, assumed familiarity and inferability, including bridging and restrictions on accessibility.

These contributions support introductory distinctions in the Model. They do not establish that the Model's small referent table captures a reader's actual mental state, that the nearest noun is a reliable resolution rule, or that every definite expression requires a preceding mention. The phone, printer and dragon examples in the Model are authored illustrations.

## Conversation and grounding

Selected prose in section 26.1 introduces turns, communicative actions, grounding, paired actions and initiative. A clarification can intervene between a request and its response. The section 26.2 distinctions separate understanding signals and rephrasing from agreement or rejection, informing the Model's distinction between understanding a contribution and accepting it.

The source labels this chapter an initial stub. Its selected passages support only an introductory explanation, not exhaustive conversation analysis. The Model's repair exchange, combined update record and comparison of understanding with truth acceptance are authored teaching synthesis.
