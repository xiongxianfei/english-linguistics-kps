# Phonology structure and distribution

## Key takeaway
Sound organization requires distinctions between structural possibility, observed forms and context-sensitive prominence.

## Summary
Selected MIT notes support syllable organization, phonotactic possibility and relational stress. The local English examples are authored applications, not data collected from these lectures.
