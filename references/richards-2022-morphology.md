# Morphological selection and alternation

## Key takeaway
A morphological analysis needs combination restrictions and lexical information as well as segmentable forms.

## Summary
Selected lecture text supports recording an affix's input and output categories and distinguishing regular from idiosyncratic alternants. It does not establish every morphological example or a complete productivity theory.

## Contribution
