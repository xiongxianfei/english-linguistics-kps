# Viewing a speech spectrogram

## Key takeaway
A spectrogram shows frequency content through time, with display choices affecting its appearance.

## Summary
The official manual supports reading spectrogram axes and shading while recognizing that a display is an analysis of a signal.

## Publication and inspection
[Manual section](https://www.fon.hum.uva.nl/praat/manual/Intro_3_1__Viewing_a_spectrogram.html), accessed 2026-10-14. The [project homepage](https://www.fon.hum.uva.nl/praat/) identifies its University of Amsterdam provenance and authors. This record attributes the documentation institutionally rather than assigning individual authorship of this page.

## Contribution
The Phonetics Model uses the time/frequency axes, energy-density shading and caution that visible darkness can change with display scaling. It does not infer a phoneme directly from a dark band.

## Nonclaims
No Praat installation, recording analysis, calibration or formant estimation was carried out. The manual's example scales are not required settings for all speakers. Its cited textbooks were not inspected for this addition.
