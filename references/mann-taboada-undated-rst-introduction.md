## Relations

The [relation definitions](https://www.sfu.ca/rst/01intro/definitions.html) describe named relationships between text spans, including evidential support and causal/explanatory relations. They support distinguishing text relations and analyzing their direction. This package uses a reduced authored inventory, not a complete RST implementation.

## Span organization

The introduction explains how relations connect spans and how smaller spans can form larger structures. It distinguishes nucleus–satellite organization from multinuclear relations, and relates proposed structures to an analyst's interpretation of a text's communicative purpose. These are theoretical representational choices, not grammatical clause labels or directly observed reader states.

This supports the Model's distinction between a relation between two clauses and evidence supplied by a larger span. Its printer-repair example is authored and was not supplied or evaluated by the source. The introductory prose also cautions against treating every text or analysis as one unproblematic tree.

## Nonclaims

The site does not certify this repository's examples or establish one universal discourse taxonomy. RST is a theoretical account with particular representational commitments. No source diagrams or full external texts are bundled.
