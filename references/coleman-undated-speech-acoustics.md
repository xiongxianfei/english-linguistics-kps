# Speech sources filters and spectra

## Key takeaway
Excitation and vocal-tract filtering describe different contributions to the speech signal.

## Summary
Selected Oxford teaching text supports the Phonetics Model's source/filter, harmonic and resonance distinctions. It supplies an introductory account rather than measurements of our examples.

## Publication and inspection
[Source-filter teaching page](https://www.phon.ox.ac.uk/source_filter_model) and [Introduction to Speech Acoustics 2](https://www.phon.ox.ac.uk/jcoleman/intro2acoustics2.htm), inspected as HTML text on 2026-10-14. The pages sit within [John Coleman's teaching materials](https://www.phon.ox.ac.uk/coleman).

## Contribution
The source/filter page distinguishes vibration and turbulence and relates tract configuration to resonances. The acoustics page distinguishes harmonic spacing from the spectral shape produced by filtering. The Model's pitch-versus-vowel-quality comparison is a bounded authored application.

## Nonclaims
No source audio, figure measurement or cited textbook inspection was performed. Illustrative frequencies, gender averages and the page's stridency classification are not adopted as universal norms. The simplified separation does not establish complete physical independence of source and filter or a full vocal-tract simulation.
