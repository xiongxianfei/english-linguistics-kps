# toPhonetics transcription converter and FAQ

## Key takeaway
The American option generates candidate broad phonemic transcriptions; it does not itself verify exact phonetic tokens.

## Summary
The [toPhonetics converter](https://tophonetics.com/) lets readers request British or American transcriptions and choose alternatives for ambiguous words. Its [FAQ](https://tophonetics.com/faq/) explains the level of transcription, conventions for rhotic symbols and vowel length, weak-form options and data origins. This is a reference for *tool behavior and notation*, not blanket evidence that any given output is linguistically correct.

## Publication identity
[Converter](https://tophonetics.com/) and [FAQ](https://tophonetics.com/faq/). Current publication date not independently established; accessed October 11, 2026.

## Inspection extent
Text on the main page and FAQ was inspected, but the form was not submitted with test words and no resulting audio or automatically generated word transcription was verified.

## Located contributions
The site calls its output phonemic, offers an American option, can show alternate readings and optional weak forms, and says its American data was initially based on the open Carnegie Mellon University Pronouncing Dictionary. Its FAQ notes that American phonemic transcriptions commonly omit vowel length marks and distinguishes broad /r/-style representation from exact [ɹ] and [ɾ] realizations.

## Limitations
Results can depend on context, homographs, dialect, database coverage and transcription conventions. A converter output is a suggestion to verify, not a measured phone or independent validation of a lexeme's pronunciation. Related products derived from CMU data may not provide independent corroboration.

## Rights
This package does not copy the site's transcription database, bulk output, interface code or playback. It links to the public tool and summarizes limited claims. Determine permissions separately before any redistribution.

## Nonclaims
No generated transcription, audio voice, API output, source corpus or independent reliability evaluation was inspected. The tool cannot by itself establish exact phonetic articulation or all accent-specific word pronunciations.
