# Context dependence and pragmatic inference

## Key takeaway
Context dependence, conversational inference and presupposition pose distinct explanatory questions.

## Summary
The inspected opening pages motivate the Pragmatics Model's separation of conventional contribution, contextual values and additional inferred content. They do not settle a unique semantic/pragmatic boundary.

## Publication and inspection
[MIT handout](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-954-pragmatics-in-linguistic-theory-fall-2006/47c7cdc6ce5a2e52764d09685e43ded8_pragmatics.pdf), PDF pages 1 and 2, accessed 2026-10-14.

## Contribution
The introduction distinguishes contextual dependence from nonsemantic inference and raises the relationship between semantic presupposition conditions and conversational background. The Model's layered representation is a local synthesis rather than the source's formal architecture.

## Nonclaims
The source's suggested sequential picture is not adopted as an established psychological processing order. Later derivations and competing theories were not reviewed for this addition. This record does not verify all deictic or interpretive examples.
