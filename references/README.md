# Source register

## Key takeaway
The source register records actual publications and inspected extent, not a blanket bibliography proving all content.

## Summary
Eleven records support selected structural conventions, linguistic distinctions and the declared pronunciation-source workflow. All knowledge objects remain provisional. A missing reference is a visible research gap; an abstract-only record is not full-text review.

## Publications

- [Knowledge Practice System authoring contract](xiongxianfei-2026-knowledge-practice-system.md) — Pinned Core 11.0.0 authoring contract and 9.1.0 validator; earlier inspection records retained; no claim of independent validation of KPS as a productivity intervention.
- [Essentials of Linguistics, first edition, McMaster University](anderson-2018-essentials-of-linguistics.md) — Selected first-edition HTML explanations of articulation, natural classes, transcription, aspiration, connected speech, suprasegmentals, morphology, syntax and meaning; precise passages and limits are recorded in the Reference. No audio/video or full-book inspection.
- [MIT ToBI tutorial](mit-2006-tobi-tutorial.md) — Selected PDF text on prominence, phrasing and accent/ending distinctions; no figure audit, audio playback or acoustic replication.
- [IPA chart and component charts on the official website](international-phonetic-association-undated-ipa-chart.md) — Official chart webpage text and visual inspection of its pulmonic-consonant, vowel and suprasegmental PNG subcharts. No audio, articulatory measurements or language-specific full inventory was examined.
- [Information Structure: Towards an integrated formal theory of pragmatics. Semantics and Pragmatics 5, article 6.](roberts-2012-information-structure.md) — Journal HTML abstract and publication metadata only. Full article PDF, detailed formal derivations and evidence sections were not inspected.
- [Rhetorical Structure Theory website introduction and relation definitions](mann-taboada-undated-rst-introduction.md) — Introduction HTML and linked relation-definitions text, including Evidence, Cause, Elaboration, Contrast and Concession. The 1988 paper cited by the website was not inspected as a full text.

- [Sounds American American IPA chart and terms](sounds-american-undated-chart-and-terms.md) — Search-indexed home chart and indexed usage terms only; no instructional videos or sound recordings played; used for pedagogical articulation orientation.
- [toPhonetics converter and FAQ](tophonetics-undated-english-transcription.md) — Inspected homepage and FAQ text; American transcriptions are candidate broad phonemic forms and have not been batch tested or verified here.
- [Interactive IPA chart](international-phonetic-association-undated-interactive-chart.md) — Official interactive page URL, associated official chart background and copyright notes; no playable tokens inspected; interactive media and the static chart have distinct licence conditions.

- [IPA diacritics and other symbols](international-phonetic-association-undated-diacritics-and-other-symbols.md) — Visual inspection of official diacritics and other-symbol PNG panels with consonant/vowel cross-checks; no audio inspected. Supports symbol modifiers and coordination, not learning effectiveness.
- [Merriam-Webster pin entry](merriam-webster-undated-pin-entry.md) — Retrieved lexical entry and its displayed dictionary notation; no audio played and the separate pronunciation-key page was inaccessible. Supports lexical identification only.

## Source rights and limits

No external full texts, charts, font files or media are distributed. The primary General American transcription policy is documented in [PRONUNCIATION](../docs/PRONUNCIATION.md). Each record includes nonclaims and attribution. The KPS source establishes its own conventions only. The first-edition Anderson mirror is not silently substituted for the uninspected second edition. Failed access to other candidate sites was not treated as successful inspection.
