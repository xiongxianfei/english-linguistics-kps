# WordNet sense relations

## Key takeaway
WordNet links senses using explicit relation types.

## Summary
Selected official documentation illustrates sense-based organization, synonym sets and named relations. It is not an exhaustive lexical theory.

## Publication and contribution
[Official project documentation](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/) was inspected on 2026-10-14. The selected sections support distinguishing sense identity from spelling and kind-of, part-of and opposite relations. The Model's examples are authored illustrations, not extracted database entries.

## Nonclaims
No database version was installed or queried, and no coverage counts or frequency estimates are used. Referenced papers were not independently read. WordNet's representational choices do not settle all lexical categories, sense boundaries or psychological storage claims. No source data or figures are redistributed.
