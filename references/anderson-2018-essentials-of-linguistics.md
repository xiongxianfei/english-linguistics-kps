## Allomorphs

The [Allomorphs chapter](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics/chapter/6-3-allomorphs/) describes alternative forms realizing one morpheme, including English plural conditioning by neighboring sound. This supports the identity-versus-form distinction. The source represents a reduced vowel differently from this package's conventional /ɪz/ notation; exact realization is accent-sensitive.

## Passive

The [Thematic Roles and Passive Sentences chapter](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics/chapter/9-4-thematic-roles-and-passive-sentences/) distinguishes grammatical positions from participant roles and compares active/passive realizations. The package's Patient versus Theme terminology is a narrower working choice, not an exact quotation of the source's role inventory.

## Aspiration

The [Aspirated Stops chapter](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics/chapter/3-5-aspirated-stops-in-english/) discusses aspiration in stressed English onset contexts and its absence in post-s clusters. This supports context-sensitive realization, not a universal VOT threshold or a claim about every token and accent.

## Adjuncts

The [Adjuncts chapter](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics/chapter/8-9-adjuncts/) presents complement versus adjunct distinctions within an X-bar account. This supports recognizing the distinction, not treating that framework or a simple deletion test as universally decisive.
