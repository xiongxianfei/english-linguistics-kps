# Collocations and idioms

## Key takeaway
Conventional combinations vary in restriction; idiomatic interpretation requires more than literal component meanings.

## Summary
Two introductory TeachingEnglish entries support the bounded distinctions used in the Lexicon Model. They do not provide a complete phraseology theory.

## Publication and contribution
[Collocation](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/professional-development/teachers/teaching-knowledge-database/c/collocation) distinguishes restricted and freer combinations. [Idioms](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/professional-development/teachers/teaching-knowledge-database/d-h/idioms) distinguishes conventional idiomatic interpretation from literal component meaning. Both were inspected on 2026-10-14.

## Nonclaims
The idiom page's tendency toward fixed wording is not adopted as a prohibition on all grammatical variation. The Model's tense/person comparison is an authored illustration. Neither page establishes corpus frequencies, dialect distributions or every local example. The Register page could not be inspected beyond a search excerpt and is not used as substantive support. No source lessons or images are redistributed.
