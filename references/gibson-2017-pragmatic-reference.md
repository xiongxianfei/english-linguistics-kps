# Pragmatics and referring expressions

## Key takeaway
Conversational expectations and interlocutor perspective help frame questions about reference and interpretation.

## Summary
Selected slides supply the four Gricean labels and distinguish shared from privileged information. This limited teaching contribution does not establish a comprehension mechanism or experimental result for the package.

## Publication and inspection
[MIT lecture slides](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/9-59j-lab-in-psycholinguistics-spring-2017/67bded72e32736334ee11313ca81725f_MIT9_59jS17_lec9.pdf), accessed 2026-10-14. PDF page 4 presents the maxims; pages 22 and 23 address perspective. Page numbers here are one-based PDF pages.

## Contribution
The Model uses the labels Quantity, Quality, Relation and Manner and the distinction between shared and privately available information. Its classroom-style examples are constructed independently.

## Nonclaims
A maxim departure does not invariably create a recoverable implicature. The Model does not adopt that strong reading of the slide shorthand. It also does not claim a universal literal-first processing sequence, reproduce restricted research figures or infer results from uninspected underlying studies.
