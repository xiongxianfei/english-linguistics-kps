# Presupposition background projection and accommodation

## Key takeaway
Presupposition projection depends on the containing structure and context; unstated background can be accommodated or challenged.

## Summary
The inspected notes support selected background, projection and accommodation distinctions. They present the Stalnaker/Karttunen approach and acknowledge unresolved issues.

## Publication and inspection
[MIT notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-954-pragmatics-in-linguistic-theory-fall-2006/ff38aef663db47f7765e0308cc4fe4e7_presupposition1.pdf), accessed 2026-10-14. The handout credits Kai von Fintel's notes and Heim 1990; those underlying works were not separately inspected here.

## Contribution
Pages 1 through 4 motivate distinguishing background from assertion and treating embedding as informative but context-sensitive. Pages 5 and 6 connect accommodation with context changes. The printer and parcel cases in the Model are authored illustrations.

## Nonclaims
No universal negation test, complete projection algorithm or full theory of dialogue is established. The record does not certify every local example or replace comparison with alternative presupposition theories.
