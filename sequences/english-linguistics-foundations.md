# English linguistics foundations

## Key takeaway
This is one recommended foundation route through the ten domains and their major interfaces; it is not an ontological or cognitively mandatory order.

## Summary
The route begins with representation and evidence distinctions, then visits word, structure, meaning, discourse and sound systems before integrating them through interface Models and a complete utterance analysis.

## Route

1. **Foundations:** expression and representation; evidence and analysis; category and function.
2. **Morphology:** morpheme; lexeme and word form; inflection; derivation; allomorphy.
3. **Lexicon:** lexical entry; sense and ambiguity; lexical selection; lexical relations; variation and register.
4. **Syntax:** phrase and clause; constituency; head and dependent; clause architecture; finiteness; ellipsis and discontinuity.
5. **Semantics:** predicate; roles; reference; compositionality; quantification and scope; plural interpretation; tense and aspect.
6. **Pragmatics:** utterance and speaker meaning; deixis; common ground; implicature; presupposition; speech act; social meaning.
7. **Discourse:** discourse referents; anaphora; relations; cohesion and coherence; dialogue and turn taking.
8. **Information Structure:** topic, focus and QUD; givenness; information packaging.
9. **Phonetics:** consonants; vowels; representation levels; connected speech; sound analysis.
10. **Phonology:** contrast and distribution; phoneme and allophone; syllables; phonotactics; stress; theoretical alternatives.
11. **Prosody:** prosody and intonation; prominence and phrasing through interface and measurement Models.
12. **Integration:** study the major interfaces, then apply Analyze complete utterance and Integrated utterance analysis.

## Use
Follow links in the generated sequence view, or enter through any domain and use prerequisites selectively. Record unresolved assumptions instead of treating completion of the route as evidence of mastery.

## Limits
Different learners and questions require different orders. The route is maintained as authored guidance, while the underlying knowledge and domain views remain independent.
