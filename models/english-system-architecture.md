## Worked interpretation

For "Maya was given the reports", Maya is an NP, Subject and Recipient. The reports is a retained Object and Theme. Context resolves the individuals and documents. A QUD can focus Maya without making her a permanent Topic. The plural exponent of reports is /s/ in the regular pronunciation.

## Assumptions and limits

The subsystem list is an authored documentation architecture, not a universally accepted taxonomy or a neural model. Two-way arrows mean possible constraint relations, not proof of symmetric direct causation among all pairs. Stage numbers provide an entry route, not ontological types.
